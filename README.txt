FREE HOSTING WITH GITHUB PAGES:
1. Sign in to https://github.com/ or create a free account.
2. Click the + menu (top right) > New repository.
3. Repository name: ram-setu-enterprises
4. Select Public. Click Create repository.
5. Click Add file > Upload files.
6. Upload index.html from this folder, then click Commit changes.
7. Open Settings > Pages.
8. Under Build and deployment, choose Deploy from a branch.
9. Branch: main; folder: /(root); click Save.
10. Wait a few minutes. The public address should be https://YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.github.io/ram-setu-enterprises/

GOOGLE SEARCH:
1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console/about and sign in with Google.
2. Add property > URL prefix, and enter your published URL exactly.
3. Complete one of the verification methods Google offers.
4. Use URL Inspection for your homepage URL and click Request indexing.
Google indexing can take days or longer and is not guaranteed. This static website collects enquiries through WhatsApp; it does not process payments or checkout.

Before launch, verify business details, GST, product specs, and contact numbers. The current site uses details supplied by the business and should be reviewed for accuracy.
